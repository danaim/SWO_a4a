Background

  • Context: Following the update of the 2020 baseline \(\alpha4\alpha\) assessment through 2024, structural enhancements were investigated to address historical data coverage and biological realism. The new catch at age matrix support using a plus group at age 9.
  • Key Objectives:
    • Temporal Scope: Evaluate sensitivity to the time-series start year by comparing the truncated period (1985–2024) against the extended historical series (1978–2024).
    • Natural Mortality (\(M\)): Assess the impact of assuming a Constant \(M\) versus an age-dependent Lorenzen \(M\) vector.
    • Fishing Mortality Dynamics: Incorporate a tensor product interaction (ti(year, age)) to capture time-varying selectivity across years and age classes.
